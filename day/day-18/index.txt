<div class="warn"><b>The four collisions worth checking before you start.</b><br/>
<b>① <span class="hz">到</span>.</b> After a verb it marks an end point (<span class="hz">我工作到五点。</span>); with no verb in front it means “arrive” (<span class="hz">我七点到公司。</span>).<br/>
<b>② <span class="hz">在</span>.</b> With nothing after the place it is the whole predicate (<span class="hz">我在家。</span>); with a verb after the place it marks where the action happens (<span class="hz">我在家里休息。</span>).<br/>
<b>③ <span class="hz">会</span>.</b> Before a skill it means “know how to” (<span class="hz">我会说汉语。</span>); before a state or event it predicts (<span class="hz">明天会很忙。</span>).<br/>
