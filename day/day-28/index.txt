<div class="warn"><b>Near-pair contrasts, taught explicitly (VI-7).</b><br/>
<b>① <span class="hz">听</span> vs <span class="hz">听见</span>.</b> Exactly the same distinction as yesterday’s <span class="hz">看</span> vs <span class="hz">看见</span>: the first is the activity, the second is the result. <span class="hz">我听了，可是我没听见。</span> — I listened, but I didn’t catch it. <b>② <span class="hz">喜欢</span> vs <span class="hz">爱</span>.</b> Both take nouns and verbs, and <span class="hz">爱</span> is stronger. <b>③ <span class="hz">它</span> vs <span class="hz">他</span> vs <span class="hz">她</span>.</b> All three are <span class="py">tā</span>. They are identical in speech and distinct only on the page — which is why today’s dictation asks for characters, not pinyin.<br/>

<b>⑤ <span class="hz">只</span> vs <span class="hz">个</span>.</b> <span class="hz">个</span> is the general-purpose measure word you have had since Day 11 and it covers almost everything. <span class="hz">只</span> is specifically for animals. <span class="hz">一只猫</span> ✓ — <span class="hz">一个猫</span> is understood but marks you as a beginner.</div>10:T1399,<p class="lead">Two points, both resting on the verb–object basics from Day 5. Neither introduces a new sentence shape; both generalise something you met yesterday.</p>
<div class="card learn">
<div class="exhead"><span class="pill">G53</span><span class="exname">Result of perception: 看见 / 听见</span><span class="extitle">difficulty: core · prerequisite: G14 (Day 5)</span></div>
<div class="exbody">
<p><b>Notice first.</b> Yesterday you learned <span class="hz">看见</span> as a single word. Today it turns out to be a pattern: <span class="hz">见</span> attaches to a verb of perception and reports that the perceiving <em>succeeded</em>.</p>
<p><b>The rule.</b> <span class="hz">看</span> and <span class="hz">听</span> are activities. Add <span class="hz">见</span> and you get the outcome. Negate the outcome with <span class="hz">没</span>, exactly as you learned yesterday.</p>
<div class="grule"><div class="pat">看 → 看见  ·  听 → 听见  ·  negative: 没看见 / 没听见</div><div class="ex">我看见他了。 / 我没看见他。 · 我听见了。 / 我没听见。</div></div>
<p><b>Two examples:</b></p>
<div class="grule"><div class="pat">我听见小朋友唱歌了。</div><div class="ex"><span class="py">Wǒ tīngjiàn xiǎopéngyou chàng gē le.</span> “I heard the children singing.” — the perception verb takes a whole clause as its object.</div></div>
<div class="grule"><div class="pat">我没听见，请再说。</div><div class="ex"><span class="py">Wǒ méi tīngjiàn, qǐng zài shuō.</span> “I didn’t catch that, please say it again.” — the single most useful sentence in this lesson.</div></div>
<p><b>Minimal contrast (GE-4):</b> <span class="hz">我听了。</span> ✓ means I listened; <span class="hz">我听见了。</span> ✓ means I heard it. And <span class="hz">我没听见了。</span> ✗ — <span class="hz">没</span> still deletes <span class="hz">了</span>, exactly as on Day 27. The rule you learned yesterday applies unchanged to today’s verb.</p>
<div class="note"><b>Why this is worth a grammar slot rather than two vocabulary entries.</b> Once you see <span class="hz">见</span> as a result marker rather than as part of a fixed word, you can predict the whole family instead of memorising it. It is the same economy that <span class="hz">好</span> + verb gives you in §2.</div>

<div class="card learn">
<div class="exhead"><span class="pill">G54</span><span class="exname">Liking with 喜欢 / 爱 + Noun or Verb</span><span class="extitle">difficulty: core · prerequisite: G14 (Day 5)</span></div>
<div class="exbody">
<p><b>Notice first.</b> Most verbs you know take a noun: <span class="hz">我吃饭。</span> <span class="hz">我看电视。</span> <span class="hz">喜欢</span> and <span class="hz">爱</span> can take a noun <em>or</em> an entire verb phrase, and that second option is what makes them powerful.</p>
<p><b>The rule.</b> Put the thing you like straight after the verb. If it is an activity, put the whole activity there — verb and object together.</p>
