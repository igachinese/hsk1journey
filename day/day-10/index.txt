<div class="exhead"><span class="pill">G21</span><span class="exname">Adverbs 也 / 都 before the verb</span><span class="extitle">difficulty: core · prerequisite: G10 (Day 4)</span></div>
<div class="exbody">
<p><b>Notice first.</b> <span class="hz">我是学生。</span> → <span class="hz">我也是学生。</span> · <span class="hz">我们是学生。</span> → <span class="hz">我们都是学生。</span></p>
