<div class="exhead"><span class="pill">G15</span><span class="exname">有 (have / there is) &amp; 没有</span><span class="extitle">difficulty: core · prerequisite: G1 (Day 1)</span></div>
<div class="exbody">
<p><b>Notice first.</b> <span class="hz">我有两个孩子。</span> · <span class="hz">我家有五口人。</span> · <span class="hz">我没有姐姐。</span></p>
<p><b>The rule.</b> <span class="hz">有</span> states possession (“have”) or existence (“there is”). What follows is usually a number + measure word + noun, exactly as on Day 7. <div class="grule"><div class="pat">Subject + 有 + (Number + MW) + Noun  ·  Subject + 没有 + Noun</div><div class="ex">我有两个孩子。 / 我没有孩子。</div></div>

<div class="grule"><div class="pat">我有两个孩子。</div><div class="ex"><span class="py">Wǒ yǒu liǎng ge háizi.</span> “I have two children.”</div></div>
<div class="grule"><div class="pat">我没有姐姐。</div><div class="ex"><span class="py">Wǒ méiyǒu jiějie.</span> “I don’t have an older sister.”</div></div>
<p><b>Minimal contrast (GE-4):</b> <span class="hz">我没有孩子。</span> ✓ vs <span class="hz">我不有孩子。</span> ✗ — and compare <span class="hz">我不是老师。</span> ✓, where <span class="hz">不</span> is still the right negator.</p>

<div class="card learn">
<div class="exhead"><span class="pill">G24</span><span class="exname">Time-when goes before the verb</span><span class="extitle">difficulty: core · prerequisite: G14 (Day 5)</span></div>
<div class="exbody">
<p><b>Notice first.</b> <span class="hz">我今天工作。</span> · <span class="hz">我明天休息。</span> · <span class="hz">今天我很高兴。</span></p>
<p><b>The rule.</b> A time-when word says <em>when</em> something happens. It goes <b>before the verb</b> — either right after the subject, or at the very front of the sentence. It never goes after the verb, which is where English puts it.</p>
<div class="grule"><div class="pat">Subject + Time + Verb  (or)  Time + Subject + Verb</div><div class="ex">我明天工作。 = 明天我工作。</div></div>
<p><b>Two examples:</b></p>
<div class="grule"><div class="pat">我今天工作。</div><div class="ex"><span class="py">Wǒ jīntiān gōngzuò.</span> “I’m working today.”</div></div>
<div class="grule"><div class="pat">我明天休息。</div><div class="ex"><span class="py">Wǒ míngtiān xiūxi.</span> “I’m resting tomorrow.”</div></div>
<p><b>Minimal contrast (GE-4):</b> <span class="hz">我明天休息。</span> ✓ vs <span class="hz">我休息明天。</span> ✗ — English says “I rest tomorrow”, Chinese says “I tomorrow rest”.</p>
