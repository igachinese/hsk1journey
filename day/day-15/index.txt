<div class="exhead"><span class="pill">G31</span><span class="exname">在 + Place (to be located)</span><span class="extitle">difficulty: core · prerequisite: G15 (Day 9)</span></div>
<div class="exbody">
<p><b>Notice first.</b> <span class="hz">我有一个家。</span> tells you what exists. <span class="hz">我在家里。</span> tells you <em>where somebody is</em> — a different question, a different verb.</p>

<div class="card learn">
<div class="exhead"><span class="pill">G32</span><span class="exname">在 + Place + Verb (where an action happens)</span><span class="extitle">difficulty: core · prerequisite: G31 — same day, taught above</span></div>

<p><b>Notice first.</b> <span class="hz">我在医院。</span> → <span class="hz">我在医院看病。</span> Add a verb after the place and the sentence stops being about where you are and starts being about what you do there.</p>
<p><b>The rule.</b> <span class="hz">在</span> + place goes <b>before the verb</b> — into the same pre-verb zone that already holds time words and <span class="hz">也 / 都</span>. English puts the place last; Chinese puts it first.</p>
<div class="grule"><div class="pat">Subject + (Time) + 在 + Place + Verb (+ Object)</div><div class="ex">我在公司上班。 / 医生在医院看病。 / 我今天在家里休息。</div></div>

<div class="card learn">
<div class="exhead"><span class="exname">Track item D15 · -n vs -ng finals</span><span class="extitle">drill material from taught vocabulary only (PT-4)</span></div>
<p><b>The difference is where the air stops.</b> For <span class="mono">-n</span>, the tongue <em>tip</em> touches the ridge behind your upper teeth and the mouth closes forward. For <span class="mono">-ng</span>, the tongue tip stays down and the <em>back</em> of the tongue rises to close the throat — the mouth stays open. English speakers usually make both, but not reliably at the end of a Chinese syllable, where the contrast changes the word.</p>
